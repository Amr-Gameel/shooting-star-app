Shooting Star App Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Shooting Star App photographs the night sky with your iPhone. It is built so that everything happens on your phone. The app does not collect, store or share any personal information, and it does not send anything over the internet.

What the app does not do

  • It has no user accounts and asks you for no personal details.
  • It does not collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports of its own. If you have allowed it, Apple may share crash reports with the developer through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy terms.
  • It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking.
  • It makes no network connections. Your pictures and settings never leave your phone unless you choose to share or export them.

What the app uses on your phone, and why

  • Camera. To take the long exposures that are stacked into a picture. The frames are processed on the phone and saved only in the app's own folder.
  • Location (while using the app). To work out which way is north and your latitude, so the app can predict how the stars move across the frame. Your location is used in the moment and is not stored, logged or sent anywhere. The app works without it, with less accurate predictions.
  • Photo library (add only). To save a finished picture to your photo library when you ask it to. The app cannot read your existing photos.
  • Motion sensors. To tell how the phone is tilted on the tripod. This information stays on the phone.

Your pictures and files

Sessions, raw frames, stacked pictures and settings are stored in the app's folder on your phone. You can see, copy and delete them with the Files app or through Finder on a Mac. Deleting the app deletes them. When you share or export a picture, it goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before that version is released.
